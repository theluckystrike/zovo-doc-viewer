# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-09)

First release.

- One custom element, `<zovo-doc-viewer>`, for DOCX, ODT, XLSX, XLS, ODS, CSV, TSV, Parquet, PDF and PPTX (beta).
- Toolbar with zoom, find, page and sheet navigation, print, download and full screen. Light, dark and auto themes.
- `load()` for a File, Blob, ArrayBuffer or URL. Events `load`, `error` and `page`.
- Zip-bomb guard (500 MB unpacked, 10,000 entries, real size counted), CSV and spreadsheet row limits.
- GPL-3.0-or-later, or a commercial licence with an offline key (COMMERCIAL.md).
